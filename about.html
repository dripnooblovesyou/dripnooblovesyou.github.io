About LakeMUN

Welcome to LakeMUN 2026! In its fifth iteration, LakeMUN now offers three unique committees covering a wide range of topics and difficulties. We aim to provide an accessible avenue for high school students across Washington state to participate in diplomacy, collaboration, and meaningful debate. We hope that all delegates are able to learn relevant skills and ideas as well as find community on May 2 at Lakeside School. Please don't hesitate to contact Heads of Conference, Sachi Tambe (sachit26@lakesideschool.org) and Aicha Sinha-Khan (aichas27@lakesideschool.org), with further questions.
