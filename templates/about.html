About LakeMUN

LakeMUN is a student-run Model United Nations conference hosted by Lakeside School in Seattle, Washington. Our mission is to empower future global leaders by providing an engaging and educational platform for students to develop their diplomacy, public speaking, and critical thinking skills.

Founded in 2020, LakeMUN has quickly become a premier MUN conference in the Pacific Northwest, attracting students from across the region. We are committed to fostering a collaborative and inclusive environment where students can explore global issues and work together to find solutions.

Heads of Conference

Sachi Tambe

Sachi Tambe is a senior at Lakeside School and is overjoyed to be serving as your Head of Conference for LakeMUN 2026! She joined Model UN as an eager freshman and entered an amazing world of diplomacy, public speaking, and leadership at her first conference. After her exhilarating time at EDUMUN 2022, she never looked back, continuing to partake in various MUN conferences and activities. When she’s not busy writing a position paper the night before it’s due, Sachi can be found reading a feel-good novel, watching action movies with her family, complaining about the cold, or practicing dance. She can’t wait to create an unforgettable experience for all of the delegates at LakeMUN this year!
